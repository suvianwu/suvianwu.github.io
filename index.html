About

I am an AI researcher at Xiaohongshu, previously at TikTok. My work lives where research meets production: business risk control, where mistakes are expensive, adversaries adapt, and decisions have to hold up at scale.

For years that domain ran on feature engineering and supervised deep models. My recent work moves it onto large-model foundations — not by swapping in a bigger classifier, but by rebuilding the stack: what the model is trained on, how it is adapted to a domain, and how it keeps improving after deployment.

The question I keep returning to: how does a model go from scoring inputs to reasoning about them, acting on them, and getting better through that interaction? That question sits behind my interest in continued pre-training, mid-training, post-training, agentic RL, and recursive self-improvement.

Research

01

How do you teach a general model a domain it has never seen?

Risk and safety domains carry knowledge no public corpus contains: adversarial patterns, platform-specific semantics, shifting policy boundaries. I work on continued pre-training and mid-training as the stage where that knowledge actually enters the model, rather than being bolted on through prompts.

02

What makes post-training produce real capability, not just better manners?

Post-training is where behavior is decided. I am interested in the alignment and reinforcement-learning recipes that move measurable capability — reward design that survives contact with messy production signals, and pipelines stable enough to run more than once.

  • Work in progress · Post-Training / Agentic RL

    Reinforcement learning for agents that act under real-world constraints

03

Can a system improve itself once it is already running?

Static models decay; adversaries do not. I work on agentic learning and recursive self-improvement — systems that use tools, observe outcomes, and fold that feedback back into their own training loop instead of waiting for the next manual refresh.

    Self-improving loops for deployed agentic systems
